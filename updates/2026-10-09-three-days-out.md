This is the last board before Monday. Both markets were pulled at 16:00Z on Friday, and the next update is the result.

**The top two held their order and the gap between them widened.** [Ariel Pakes](/candidates/ariel-pakes/) is 23 cents on Kalshi and 21 on Polymarket, where he was 26 and 22 on Tuesday. He traded down to 20 on Kalshi overnight into Friday and was bought straight back to 23 on a 543-contract buy in the US morning. [Susan Athey](/candidates/susan-athey/) took the week's real hit. Kalshi sold her from 21 to 12 across twenty-five trades on Wednesday, and Polymarket had her displayed near 10 on Thursday. Buyers returned on both: a 525-contract buy at 13 and a 583-contract buy at 15 on Kalshi brought her back to 16, and Polymarket has her at 14.5, up four on the day. She still carries the largest open interest on Kalshi, 7,051 contracts. So the money's read three days out is Pakes first, Athey second, and a seven-point gap where there was a six-point gap on Tuesday and a reversed one a week before that.

**Kalshi added three names on Wednesday, and somebody bought all three at pennies on Friday morning.** [Emmanuel Saez](/candidates/emmanuel-saez/), [Thomas Piketty](/candidates/thomas-piketty/) and [Sendhil Mullainathan](/candidates/sendhil-mullainathan/) were listed at 17:00Z on October 7, which takes Kalshi's book from nine names to twelve. The first prints were small. Then between 10:00Z and 10:50Z on Friday, which is noon in Stockholm and three in the morning in California, about 2,000 contracts of Piketty were bought at 2 and 3 cents, 2,000 of Mullainathan at 1 and 2 cents, including a 967-lot and a 500-lot in the same minute, and a 500-lot of Saez at 4. Piketty's open interest is 2,624 after two days, which puts him fifth on the board by that measure, ahead of [Richard Blundell](/candidates/richard-blundell/) and [Robert Barro](/candidates/robert-barro/). It is cheap exposure to an inequality prize that no forecaster on our board has called this year, and it is the same shape as the 1,400 contracts of [Luigi Zingales](/candidates/luigi-zingales/) bought at 4 and 5 on Tuesday. That position was sold on Thursday: a 2,000-lot and a 1,000-lot hit the bid at 3 three minutes apart, and Zingales now prints at 1. [Matthew Rabin](/candidates/matthew-rabin/) got the same treatment, sold from 5 to 2 on Wednesday in 1,000-, 800- and 600-contract sales inside one minute, with 3,141 more contracts sold Friday. Rabin now has the second-largest open interest on the book, 5,355 contracts, almost all of it on the no side. We can see the pattern and not the hand: someone is cycling through the longshots, buying each at a few cents and selling the last one to pay for the next.

**Polymarket doubled.** $33,000 on opening day is $71,000 now, with $55,000 of liquidity resting, and the book has moved. [Michael Woodford](/candidates/michael-woodford/) went from 8 to 11.5 and is third on Polymarket. [David Autor](/candidates/david-autor/) went from 6.5 to 8.5, up three on Friday alone, with $1,392 traded in the last day. Blundell held near 10. [Partha Dasgupta](/candidates/partha-dasgupta/) slipped from 9.9 to 5.5 on almost no trading, and [Victor Chernozhukov](/candidates/victor-chernozhukov/) fell from 9.5 to 3.7 as his wide opening book tightened. Woodford, Autor, [Lawrence Katz](/candidates/lawrence-katz/) and [Hal Varian](/candidates/hal-varian/) are the names Polymarket prices and Kalshi does not list, so the markets' disagreement on third place is partly a disagreement about who is on the ballot. Where both list a name they are within two points of each other on every one.

**Nothing new from the forecasters.** [Prophet Arena](https://www.prophetarena.co/events/KXNOBELECON-26) still forecasts the original nine Kalshi names and has not published a number we could read since Tuesday, so the board carries its Athey 37.7 and Pakes 20.6. Scott Cunningham's October 1 update is still behind its paywall. The [Tomas Bata](https://fame.utb.cz/en/news-events/who-will-win-the-nobel-prize-in-economic-sciences-this-year/) contest closes to entries on Saturday, and its tally and HSE's both arrive with the prize, so both sources enter the board in the result update.

**What it does to the Power Rankings.** Not much at the top, which is the point of a composite: Pakes 29.8, Athey 27.2, both down a little as the markets took money off the table, the order unchanged. Woodford's Polymarket rise moves him to third at 20.3, past [Steven Berry](/candidates/steven-berry/), who has three forecasters and no market. Varian and [Sidney Winter](/candidates/sidney-winter/) hold fifth and sixth on Clarivate plus Polymarket. Autor climbs two to seventh on his Friday bid, and Barro slips to a tie with Katz at eighth as Kalshi sold him from 12 to 7. Below the line, Blundell holds thirteenth, Dasgupta falls two to sixteenth, and Rabin falls two to twentieth. Saez and Piketty enter the top twenty-five at twenty-first and twenty-second on their new Kalshi prices, and Mullainathan sits below them at 2 cents.
