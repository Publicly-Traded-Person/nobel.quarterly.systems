**The work.** Absorptive capacity with Cohen, plus the rugged-landscape model of how organizations adapt and work with James March on the myopia of learning.

**The case for.** Two of the most cited papers in organizational science, and the adaptation work gave economics a way to think about firms searching in the dark.

**The case against.** His influence lives in management journals, not economics, and the committee rewarded innovation in 2018 and 2025. A joint prize with Cohen is the only obvious route.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Levinthal's landscapes show an organization making local moves to climb a hill it cannot see, which is the shareholder-run life in one picture.
