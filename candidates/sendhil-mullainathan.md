**The work.** The behavioral economics of poverty and scarcity, the resume audit study with Bertrand, and more recently machine learning as a tool for economic prediction and for auditing human decisions like bail.

**The case for.** He connects behavioral economics, discrimination, and AI in a way no one else does, and the scarcity work reframed how development economists think about the poor.

**The case against.** Kahneman, Thaler, and the 2019 development trio already collected much of the behavioral and poverty credit, and his output is spread across fields rather than concentrated in one.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Mullainathan argues scarcity taxes attention and bends decisions; KmikeyM outsourced his decisions to shareholders partly to get the bandwidth back.
