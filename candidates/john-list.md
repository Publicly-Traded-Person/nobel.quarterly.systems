**The work.** He took economics out of the lab and into flea markets, charities, and schools, turning field experiments into a mainstream method.

**The case for.** Field experiments now run everywhere from ride-share apps to school districts, and List is the name attached to the method.

**The case against.** Experiments were rewarded in 2019 with Banerjee, Duflo, and Kremer, and he is still in his fifties; the committee rarely revisits a method this fast.

**The KmikeyM angle.** KmikeyM is a running field experiment with a sample size of one, and List's obsession with whether lab results survive contact with real stakes is the whole question.
