**The work.** The P in BLP, plus the Ericson-Pakes framework for dynamic competition, where firms invest, enter and exit while watching each other, plus the Olley-Pakes fix for production function estimation.

**The case for.** The most decorated of the three, with a BBVA Frontiers of Knowledge Award, and his name is on the demand, supply and dynamics tools of modern IO.

**The case against.** Tirole took the field's last medal in 2014, and the committee has yet to reward empirical IO as a body of work rather than a theory.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Ericson-Pakes models firms that keep investing because rivals might. Mike invests in himself because shareholders are watching, the same game with one player and an audience.
