**The work.** He made entrepreneurship a serious empirical field. His knowledge spillover theory says new firms form when someone inside a big organization sees an idea the organization will not use, and walks out the door with it.

**The case for.** Entrepreneurship is the one part of the economy every politician talks about and no laureate has been honored for.

**The case against.** Mokyr, Aghion and Howitt took the innovation and growth prize in 2025, so that slot is spent. Small business research reads as management in Stockholm.

**The KmikeyM angle.** KmikeyM is the knowledge spillover theory run on a single person: an idea no employer would fund, spun out as a public company whose only asset is the founder.
