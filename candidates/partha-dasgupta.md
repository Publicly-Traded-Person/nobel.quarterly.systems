**The work.** Putting nature on the balance sheet: natural capital as wealth that depreciates, the economics of biodiversity, and the 2021 Dasgupta Review commissioned by the UK Treasury.

**The case for.** Nordhaus got climate in 2018, but the broader question of valuing ecosystems has never been rewarded, and Dasgupta is its founding figure with fifty years of theory behind him.

**The case against.** He was born in 1942 and the committee has passed on him for decades. Environmental economics just had its turn, and the Review is a report, not a paper.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Dasgupta's whole argument is that an unpriced asset gets run down; KmikeyM priced the human asset precisely so someone would notice the depreciation.
