**The work.** With Helpman, innovation-driven growth and the political economy of trade policy. Later, offshoring and the "trading tasks" view of global supply chains with Rossi-Hansberg.

**The case for.** Trading tasks made him the economist of the supply-chain era, and Grossman and Helpman remains the reference for how lobbying shapes tariffs.

**The case against.** Every prize adjacent to his work has been handed out: Krugman 2008, Romer 2018, Aghion and Howitt 2025. Joint work with Helpman means a split citation at best.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Trading tasks says you offshore the pieces of a job, not the job. KmikeyM offshores the pieces of a life to shareholders, one vote at a time.
