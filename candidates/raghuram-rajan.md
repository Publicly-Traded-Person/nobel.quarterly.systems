**The work.** Banking and corporate finance theory with Diamond and Zingales, the 2005 Jackson Hole warning that finance was making the world riskier, and the book Fault Lines on the 2008 crisis.

**The case for.** He called the crisis before it happened, then ran India's central bank. Academics with that policy record get a second look.

**The case against.** Douglas Diamond, his most frequent co-author, won in 2022 without him. Born in 1963, he has time, and a public-intellectual profile sometimes reads as less academic than the record is.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Rajan's theme is that finance concentrates risk and power in ways the governed cannot see, which is the problem KmikeyM's disclosure regime exists to solve.
