**The work.** The political economy of labor market institutions: why Europe's employment protections persist even when they lower employment, who votes for them and why. Also growth theory with unemployment, and a line of work on the economics of paternalism.

**The case for.** Tenth on Richard Tol's list, with a model probability of 2.1 percent. A European labor-institutions prize would be a first.

**The case against.** No market, no Clarivate mention, no forecaster. Tenth on a list whose first name has a one-in-twenty chance is a long way from Stockholm.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Saint-Paul asks why people vote for rules that cost them. KmikeyM shareholders have voted Mike into a Republican voter registration and a vasectomy. The rules cost him; they voted anyway.
