**The work.** Absorptive capacity, written with Levinthal in 1990: a firm's own research spending is what lets it recognize and use knowledge from outside.

**The case for.** The paper is among the most cited in management science, and it changed how economists think about R&D spillovers and why they are not free.

**The case against.** It is a management concept as much as an economics one, and innovation got Romer in 2018, the year Cohen was named, then Aghion, Howitt, and Mokyr in 2025.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Absorptive capacity says you only profit from outside advice if you have done the work to understand it. A person governed by shareholders lives that every vote.
