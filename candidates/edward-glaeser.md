**The work.** Cities as engines of growth: why people cluster, why housing regulation chokes the most productive cities, and why density beats sprawl. Triumph of the City is the popular version.

**The case for.** Urban economics has never been rewarded outright, and Glaeser is the field's public face and its most cited living practitioner. Housing is now a first-order political fight in every rich country.

**The case against.** Krugman's 2008 prize already touched economic geography, and the committee may see urban as a subfield rather than a body of theory.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Glaeser's thesis is that proximity makes people more productive; KmikeyM's shareholders have spent years voting on where one man should live.
