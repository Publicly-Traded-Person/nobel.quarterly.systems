**The work.** With Grossman, the modern theory of trade under increasing returns, innovation-driven endogenous growth, and the political economy of protection in "Protection for Sale."

**The case for.** Grossman and Helpman is the standard toolkit for trade, growth, and lobbying. Krugman's 2008 prize cited work Helpman coauthored.

**The case against.** Krugman took the trade prize and Aghion and Howitt took the innovation-growth prize in 2025. The room he would enter has been cleared out twice.

**The KmikeyM angle.** "Protection for Sale" models a government auctioning policy to lobbies. KmikeyM sells governance by the share, which is the same auction with the price posted.
