**The work.** The financial accelerator with Bernanke and Gilchrist, which shows how balance-sheet damage amplifies a downturn, and the Clarida, Galí, and Gertler monetary policy framework.

**The case for.** The 2008 crisis played out like a Bernanke and Gertler paper. He supplied the theory that turned a banking panic into a macro model.

**The case against.** Bernanke already won in 2022 for banks and crises, and Gertler was not named alongside him. The committee has spent that citation.

**The KmikeyM angle.** The financial accelerator says a small hit to net worth becomes a large hit to credit. A bad month for Mike knocks the share price, which knocks the next raise, which is the accelerator running on one person.
