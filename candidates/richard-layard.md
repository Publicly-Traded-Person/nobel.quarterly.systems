**The work.** Layard argued that governments should measure and maximize well-being directly rather than income, then went and did it. His lobbying built Britain's national talking therapies program and got well-being into official statistics.

**The case for.** Happiness economics went from fringe to policy agenda in twenty years, and he is its most effective evangelist.

**The case against.** He is 92. The field's founder, Richard Easterlin, died in 2024, and the committee has never treated subjective well-being as a result rather than a survey.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Layard's question is what a society should actually maximize. KmikeyM shareholders face the same choice every vote: the share price, or the man.
