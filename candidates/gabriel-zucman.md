**The work.** Following hidden wealth through offshore tax havens, estimating how much the rich shelter, and building the distributional national accounts that show who gets what share of growth. Clark Medal, 2023.

**The case for.** He turned tax evasion from anecdote into a measurable stock and wrote the playbook for a global minimum tax on billionaires.

**The case against.** Born in 1986. Nobody wins this prize at 40, and his most political proposals are still moving through legislatures rather than through the literature.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Zucman hunts for wealth that hides from its owners' governments; KmikeyM is the one asset whose owner cannot hide from his own shareholders.
