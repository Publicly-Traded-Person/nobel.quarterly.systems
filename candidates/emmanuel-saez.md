**The work.** Top income shares from tax records, optimal tax theory, and the modern accounting of who actually pays taxes in the United States. Clark Medal, 2009.

**The case for.** The Piketty-Saez top income series is the workhorse dataset behind two decades of inequality research, and Clark medalists convert to Nobels at a strong rate.

**The case against.** A joint prize with Piketty and Zucman is the natural form, and the committee has to decide whether the trio's advocacy on wealth taxes counts against them.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Saez studies what the top 1 percent keep; KmikeyM publishes his own accounts to shareholders every quarter, so the top 100 percent of him is disclosed.
