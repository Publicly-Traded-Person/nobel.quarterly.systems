**The work.** The Dixit-Stiglitz model of monopolistic competition, the foundation of new trade theory and new growth theory, plus the investment-under-uncertainty framework with Pindyck.

**The case for.** Dixit-Stiglitz is one of the most used models in economics. Krugman's prize, Romer's prize, and Aghion and Howitt's all ran on it.

**The case against.** He is 82 and Stiglitz already won in 2001 for something else. The committee has had three chances to reward the model and rewarded its users instead.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Dixit and Pindyck showed that an irreversible commitment under uncertainty carries option value you destroy by acting early. Every KmikeyM vote on a life decision is priced the same way.
