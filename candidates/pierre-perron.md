**The work.** Perron showed that a single structural break, like the 1929 crash or the 1973 oil shock, can make a well behaved series look like a random walk. Testing for breaks is now standard.

**The case for.** He is the youngest of the 2020 unit root trio and still publishing, so he could carry the field's medal alone.

**The case against.** A refinement to somebody else's test is a hard sell for a prize that wants a whole idea, and the field has been rewarded already.

**The KmikeyM angle.** A person's price series is all structural breaks: a layoff, a move, a new relationship. Perron would say test for the break before you call it noise.
