**The work.** A Harvard economics PhD, a Wall Street career, vice-president of Argentina's central bank under Macri, then head of the Council of Economic Advisers in Javier Milei's first years. His published economics is thin; his policy footprint is the Argentine stabilization of 2024 and 2025.

**The case for.** None in the academic sense. He is here because Polymarket listed him on its opening day, October 6, and a few hundred dollars of Argentine enthusiasm priced him at seven cents, above Hal Varian.

**The case against.** The prize goes to economic science. Reidel has not published it. The market line is a bet on a meme, not a committee.

**The KmikeyM angle.** A prediction market will list anyone a crowd wants to bet on. That is not a flaw; it is the product. KmikeyM has been that product since 2008.
