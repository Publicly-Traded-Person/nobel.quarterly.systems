**The work.** Zingales works on corporate governance, the political economy of finance, and the question of who capitalism actually serves. With Rajan he wrote *Saving Capitalism from the Capitalists*; on his own, *A Capitalism for the People* and a long run of papers on trust, culture, and the finance-growth link.

**The case for.** Kalshi lists him, and the committee has been drifting toward political economy. The governance work is widely cited and unusually readable.

**The case against.** He is a public intellectual as much as a researcher, his best-known co-author Rajan is a separate ticket, and the committee rarely rewards podcasts.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Zingales asks whether shareholders should run things and whether they do; KmikeyM is one person who let them, so his work is the closest thing to a literature review of the experiment.
