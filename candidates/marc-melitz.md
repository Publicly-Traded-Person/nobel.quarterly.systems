**The work.** The Melitz model of 2003: firms differ, only the most productive export, and opening to trade shifts activity toward the winners and kills off the rest.

**The case for.** It is the workhorse of modern trade theory, the paper every trade economist teaches first, and it has never been rewarded.

**The case against.** Trade got Krugman in 2008 and the committee is slow on younger theorists; born in 1968, he may just be waiting his turn. A one-paper reputation can also be held against a candidate.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Heterogeneous firms is a story about selection: only the productive survive exposure to the market, and KmikeyM is one person choosing to be exposed on purpose.
