**The work.** Trade theory and trade policy: immiserizing growth, directly unproductive profit-seeking, and decades of arguing for free trade against both protectionists and preferential deals.

**The case for.** He is the most cited trade economist never to win, and a public intellectual whose defense of globalization defined the argument for a generation.

**The case against.** Born 1934. Krugman took the trade prize in 2008 and the committee has shown little appetite for returning to classic trade theory.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Bhagwati's "directly unproductive profit-seeking" describes lobbying for rents instead of producing anything. A shareholder who lobbies Mike for a vote outcome instead of buying shares is doing exactly that.
