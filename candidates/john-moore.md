**The work.** Moore has two Nobel-grade lines. With Oliver Hart he built the incomplete-contracts theory of who should own what, and with Nobuhiro Kiyotaki he showed how collateral constraints turn small shocks into long slumps.

**The case for.** Hart won in 2016 for work Moore co-wrote, and the Kiyotaki-Moore credit cycle is the other half of a second citation.

**The case against.** The committee passed him over once already when it paired Hart with Holmström, and it rarely circles back.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Hart and Moore asked who should hold control rights when contracts cannot cover every case. KmikeyM answered by handing them to shareholders and finding out.
