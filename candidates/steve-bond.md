**The work.** Dynamic panel data methods, above all the Arellano-Bond estimator and the Blundell-Bond system estimator, plus applied work on investment and corporate taxation.

**The case for.** His name is on two of the most used estimators in applied economics, and they are workhorses across the discipline.

**The case against.** He is the second name on both papers, his applied work is respected but not committee material, and econometrics was rewarded in 2021.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Panel methods separate what is fixed about a person from what changes over time, which is the split every KmikeyM shareholder is arguing over.
