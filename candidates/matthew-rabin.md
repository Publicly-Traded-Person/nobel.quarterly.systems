**The work.** Formal models of psychology in economics: fairness as intentions, the calibration theorem on risk aversion, present-biased procrastination with O'Donoghue, and projection bias.

**The case for.** He is the theorist who made behavioral economics rigorous enough to model. The calibration theorem is a short argument that still embarrasses expected utility.

**The case against.** Thaler's 2017 prize covered much of this territory and Rabin was not on it. At 63 he is young by committee standards and can wait.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Present bias says people know the right long-term move and still defer it. KmikeyM exists because Mike outsourced the willpower problem to people whose money is on the line.
