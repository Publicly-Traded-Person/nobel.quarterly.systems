**The work.** Partial identification: the discipline of saying what the data can and cannot tell you, and reporting a range instead of faking a point estimate. Also the reflection problem in social interactions.

**The case for.** His insistence on honest uncertainty has shaped how careful applied work gets done, and the committee likes methodologists with one clear idea.

**The case against.** Bounds are respected more than used. Born in 1948, and econometrics got its turn in 2021 with Card, Angrist, and Imbens for a rival approach.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Shareholders vote on Mike's life with thin data, and Manski's rule fits: say what you know, admit the range, and do not pretend the point estimate is real.
