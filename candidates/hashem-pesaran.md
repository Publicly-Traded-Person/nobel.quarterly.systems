**The work.** Pesaran built the tools applied economists reach for when data comes in panels: a test for whether countries or firms move together, an estimator that handles it, and the bounds test for long-run relationships. His Global VAR ties national economies into one system.

**The case for.** Every cross-country macro paper of the last twenty years runs at least one Pesaran procedure.

**The case against.** Tool builders get cited, not crowned, and he would split with Hendry and Phillips, who each have a cleaner single line.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Cross-sectional dependence means units that look independent secretly move together, which is every KmikeyM vote once the shareholders read the same Discord.
