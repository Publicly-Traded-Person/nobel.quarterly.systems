**The work.** Centuries of wealth and income inequality reconstructed from tax data, the claim that returns on capital outrun growth, and Capital in the Twenty-First Century, an economics book that sold like a novel.

**The case for.** He built the World Inequality Database with Saez and Zucman and put inequality back at the center of the discipline.

**The case against.** The committee is wary of political lightning rods, the theory in Capital took heavy fire, and the data work is shared with co-authors who would split a prize.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Piketty says capital compounds faster than labor; KmikeyM turned his labor into capital and sold it to shareholders, which is either the fix or the problem.
