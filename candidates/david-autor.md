**The work.** The China Shock, which measured what import competition did to American manufacturing towns, and the polarization of the labor market as routine jobs got automated away.

**The case for.** He turned trade and technology from theory into hard local evidence, and the findings reshaped how both parties talk about trade. The AI-and-jobs debate starts with his work.

**The case against.** Card's 2021 prize covered labor empirics, and a China Shock prize would have to include Dorn and Hanson, which crowds the three seats if Katz is also in.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Autor tracks what happens to a worker when the market decides his skills are obsolete; KmikeyM lets the market vote on his skills before the obsolescence arrives.
