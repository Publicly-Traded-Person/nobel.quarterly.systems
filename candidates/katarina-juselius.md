**The work.** The cointegrated VAR as an applied research program: the 1990 Johansen-Juselius paper on money demand and a career of pushing the method into real macro data.

**The case for.** She made Johansen's mathematics usable by applied economists, and her book on the cointegrated VAR is the standard practitioner's reference.

**The case against.** The 2003 prize already covered cointegration, and a joint prize with Johansen is the only route. Born in 1943.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Juselius argues you should let the data speak before imposing theory on it, which is roughly what a shareholder market does to a person's plans.
