**The work.** The race between education and technology, the Katz-Murphy model of wage inequality, and the long-run Moving to Opportunity study. He has also edited the Quarterly Journal of Economics for decades.

**The case for.** Half of modern labor economics traces back to a Katz paper or a Katz student. The wage inequality work is foundational and has aged well.

**The case against.** His best-known books are joint with Claudia Goldin, who already won in 2023, and the committee may feel that ground has been covered.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Katz says wages track the race between skills and machines; KmikeyM has run the same race in public, with shareholders holding the stopwatch.
