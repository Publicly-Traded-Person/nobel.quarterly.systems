**The work.** The resume audit study with Mullainathan that showed identical resumes with Black-sounding names got fewer callbacks, plus research on CEO pay, gender gaps at the top, and corporate governance.

**The case for.** The audit study is a landmark in discrimination economics and a model of how to make a hard thing measurable. Her range across labor and corporate finance is unusual.

**The case against.** The committee just rewarded a labor-market empiricist in Goldin, and the discrimination work may be read as one famous paper rather than a program.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Bertrand's CEO pay work asks whether executives are rewarded for luck; KmikeyM's shareholders can read the ledger and vote on whether their CEO got lucky.
