**The work.** The alternating-offers bargaining model of 1982, models of bounded rationality, and a long public argument that economic theory is a set of fables, not a forecasting machine.

**The case for.** The bargaining model is in every graduate curriculum, and his bounded-rationality work anticipated much of behavioral economics.

**The case against.** He says in public that theory should not be used to steer policy, which makes him an awkward laureate, and pure theory has had a thin decade at the committee. Born in 1951.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Rubinstein's model splits a pie between two impatient parties, and the KmikeyM share price is a running bargain between Mike's patience and his shareholders'.
