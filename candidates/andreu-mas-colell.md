**The work.** Mas-Colell is a general equilibrium theorist and, with Whinston and Green, the author of *Microeconomic Theory* (1995), the graduate textbook that trained every economist under 55. His research put differential topology to work on the existence and structure of equilibria and on markets with many commodities.

**The case for.** He is on Kalshi's list, which is why he is here, and the committee has honored equilibrium theory before. The textbook alone makes him one of the most-read economists alive.

**The case against.** Pure theory prizes have thinned out, his most cited work is a textbook, and his years as a Catalan government minister put him outside the research stream for a decade.

**The KmikeyM angle.** General equilibrium is the theory of what happens when everyone trades everything at once, which is a description of the KmikeyM cap table on a vote day.
