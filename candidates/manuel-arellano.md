**The work.** The Arellano-Bond estimator, the standard method for dynamic panel data when you follow many units over a few periods.

**The case for.** Among the most cited econometrics papers ever written, used across labor, growth, and finance. The committee has rewarded tools of this kind before, with Heckman and Granger.

**The case against.** Econometrics got Card, Angrist, and Imbens in 2021, and a single estimator, however cited, is a thin story next to the identification revolution.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Arellano built tools for learning from units observed over time, which is the only data KmikeyM will ever produce: one man, many periods.
