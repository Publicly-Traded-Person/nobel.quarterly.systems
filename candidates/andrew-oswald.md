**The work.** With David Blanchflower he documented the U-shaped curve of happiness over life, bottoming out in the late forties, and priced unemployment, marriage and commuting in happiness terms.

**The case for.** The youngest of the 2022 happiness trio and the one with the largest empirical footprint, so a prize for the field could land on him.

**The case against.** Easterlin, the founder, died in 2024, and a prize for a survey question is a stretch for a committee that prefers mechanisms.

**The KmikeyM angle.** The Blanchflower-Oswald curve bottoms out around 47. Mike is 49, so his shareholders hold an asset on the theory that the curve is real and he is past the trough.
