**The work.** Labor supply, consumer demand, and the microeconometrics of household behavior, much of it built on British survey data at the Institute for Fiscal Studies.

**The case for.** He is the standard reference on how taxes and benefits change how people work, and the empirical toolkit he built runs through most of applied public economics.

**The case against.** Labor was rewarded in 2021 with Card, Angrist, and Imbens, and his output has no single headline result the committee can put on a poster.

**The KmikeyM angle.** His whole career is measuring how a person responds to incentives, which is the question every shareholder vote on Mike's behavior is trying to answer.
