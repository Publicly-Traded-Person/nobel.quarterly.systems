**The work.** Murphy is the Chicago empiricist behind the skill-biased story of wage inequality, the Becker-Murphy theory of rational addiction, and the big-push model of industrialization with Shleifer and Vishny. He took the Clark Medal in 1997 and a MacArthur in 2005.

**The case for.** Inequality is the committee's favorite subject this decade, and Murphy's data work on it came first.

**The case against.** The work sprawls across five fields, which makes a one-line citation hard, and his most famous co-author, Becker, already has a medal.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Becker and Murphy argued addicts plan ahead. KmikeyM is a bet that a man with shareholders plans better than one without.
