**The work.** The New Keynesian model in its modern form: the New Keynesian Phillips curve with Gertler, the textbook central bank economists learned from, and the finding that technology shocks lower hours worked.

**The case for.** The Clarida, Galí, and Gertler framework is the model central banks actually use. If the committee ever rewards the 2000s monetary consensus, he sits at its center.

**The case against.** That consensus took damage after 2008 and again after the 2021 inflation miss. A three-way split with Taylor and Gertler dilutes any individual case.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Galí's models are about credible commitment: a central bank that binds itself beats one that improvises, which is the whole argument for letting shareholders bind Mike.
