**The work.** Posner's Economic Analysis of Law, from 1973, turned law and economics from a Chicago seminar into a field, arguing common law rules tend toward efficiency. He then applied it from the bench, on the Seventh Circuit from 1981 to 2017.

**The case for.** He is the most cited legal scholar of his century, with influence that runs through courts as well as journals.

**The case against.** He is a judge, not an economist, he is in his late eighties, and the committee has never crowned law and economics as such.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Posner judged legal rules by whether they maximize wealth, and KmikeyM's bylaws get judged the same way, by shareholders watching the price.
