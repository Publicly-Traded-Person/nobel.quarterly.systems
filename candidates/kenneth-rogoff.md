**The work.** The conservative central banker model (1985), which says hire someone who hates inflation more than you do, plus the Obstfeld-Rogoff textbook that defines open economy macro, plus This Time Is Different with Reinhart.

**The case for.** Three bodies of work that each stand alone, plus former IMF chief economist and chess grandmaster.

**The case against.** The crisis half was covered by the 2022 prize, and central bank independence is a topic the committee has stepped around for forty years.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Rogoff's trick, hire a hawk to tie your own hands, is exactly what a person does by handing life decisions to shareholders who care less about his comfort than he does.
