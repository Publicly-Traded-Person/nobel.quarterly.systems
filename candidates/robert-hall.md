**The work.** The random-walk theory of consumption, models of labor market flows in recessions, and long service chairing the NBER committee that dates American business cycles.

**The case for.** He decides when US recessions start and end, his consumption paper is a classic, and the flat tax he wrote with Rabushka shaped decades of policy debate.

**The case against.** Born in 1943, and macro's turn came in 2022 with Bernanke, Diamond, and Dybvig. His work spans too many topics for a tidy citation.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Hall's consumption result says people smooth spending against expected lifetime income, which is what a shareholder-managed person does when the market sets his budget.
