**The work.** Corporate finance: the pecking order theory of how firms choose between debt and equity, the debt overhang result, the phrase "real options," and the Brealey-Myers textbook.

**The case for.** Pecking order and debt overhang are in every MBA curriculum, and corporate finance has not been rewarded since Modigliani and Miller.

**The case against.** Born in 1940. Finance got Fama, Hansen, and Shiller in 2013, and co-nominee Michael Jensen died in 2024, so the trio Clarivate named no longer exists.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Pecking order says firms sell equity last, only when they cannot borrow. KmikeyM sold equity first, on purpose, which makes Myers the theory Mike inverted.
