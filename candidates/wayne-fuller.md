**The work.** The Fuller half of Dickey-Fuller, plus decades of work on survey sampling and measurement error that shaped how statistical agencies handle their data.

**The case for.** The unit root test is one of the most cited tools in empirical economics, and Clarivate named him alongside Dickey and Perron.

**The case against.** He was born in 1931. The committee rarely hands the prize to statisticians, and the time series slot went to Engle and Granger in 2003.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Measurement error is the whole problem with pricing a person: the market prints a number every day, and what that number measures is anyone's guess.
