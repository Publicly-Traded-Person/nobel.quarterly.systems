**The work.** Intertemporal choice, the hot-cold empathy gap, projection bias, and building behavioral economics into a research program at Carnegie Mellon.

**The case for.** Much of what people mean by behavioral economics traces to his papers on visceral influences and present bias. He is a co-founder of the field in the plain sense.

**The case against.** Thaler took the behavioral prize in 2017, the year Loewenstein was named, and the committee rarely doubles back within a decade. Output spread across psychology and economics journals also hurts.

**The KmikeyM angle.** The hot-cold empathy gap is the reason shareholders exist: cold-state voters decide what a hot-state Mike is allowed to do.
