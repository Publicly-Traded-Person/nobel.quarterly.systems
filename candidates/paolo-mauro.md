**The work.** The 1995 paper that first showed, across countries, that corruption cuts investment and growth. It launched the empirical literature on institutions and governance.

**The case for.** The paper is one of the most cited in development economics and gave policymakers a number to point at. The committee likes work that opened a field.

**The case against.** He spent his career at the IMF rather than in academia, and one landmark paper is a thin basis for a Nobel. Acemoglu, Johnson and Robinson took the institutions prize in 2024.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Mauro measured what happens when the people running a system extract from it; KmikeyM's design makes the person running the system the one extracted from, on purpose.
