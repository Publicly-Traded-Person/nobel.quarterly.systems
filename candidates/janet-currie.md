**The work.** How early childhood conditions, from pollution exposure to Medicaid coverage to prenatal health, shape a child's later life, measured with administrative data at scale.

**The case for.** She built the economics of child health almost from scratch, the findings drive policy, and the committee has yet to reward health economics directly.

**The case against.** Heckman's 2000 prize already touched early childhood investment, and her work reads as a program of careful findings rather than one landmark result.

**The KmikeyM angle.** Currie shows the returns on a person get set before anyone is paying attention; KmikeyM's shareholders bought in when he was already 30, so the early rounds went unpriced.
